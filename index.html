How Pragati Connect Works

Connecting demand with specialized execution capacity in three seamless steps.

Step 1: Register Your Profile

Contractors showcase machinery, manpower, past projects, and CPWD/ISO credentials. Companies configure procurement needs.

Step 2: Discover Opportunities

Contractors filter tenders by trade, budget, and location. EPC companies search equipment availability and verified contractor profiles.

Step 3: Connect & Grow

Send direct enquiries, negotiate timelines and rates, submit formal bids, and award subcontract packages with zero middlemen friction.
